# Training Principles — DailyRepsGuy Framework

The programming methodology used by this workspace. Source: DailyRepsGuy blueprint.

---

## Core Format

Each strength session: **5 movements in a 20-minute AMRAP circuit**
- 1 movement per body part slot: Arms/Chest, Legs, Abs, Back
- 1 Focus movement — heavier or more demanding version in one slot
- Rest: 10–45 seconds between movements
- Track per session: rounds completed, final-round reps per movement, felt-difficulty 1–5

**Target:** 5+ rounds in 20 minutes.

---

## Phase System

Each movement has its own phase, set independently.

| Phase | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Phase 1 | Beginner — bodyweight or light load, basic pattern |
| Phase 2 | Intermediate — added load or more complex variation |
| Phase 3 | Advanced — heavier load or demanding variation |

A user can be Phase 3 on pulls and Phase 1 on overhead presses. Phases are per-movement, not per-person.

---

## Progression Rules

**Phase up when:** +2 reps on the final set for 2 sessions in a row, OR total reps in 20 min are +10–20% over baseline. Pick the smaller change: phase up OR add reps — not both at once.

**Phase down when:** form breaks on any rep, can't complete a single round, or pain (not soreness — pain). Drop one phase on that movement only.

**Stalled:** Reps flat for 3 consecutive sessions of the same body part = flag for weekly structural review. Don't keep trying the same thing.

---

## 2-Week Cycle

| Week | Goal |
|------|------|
| Week 1 — Calibration | Complete each circuit. Log rounds and final-round reps. Don't max out — measure. |
| Week 2 — Push | Same structure. Beat Week 1 by 10–20% rounds or reps. If you can hit +2 final-round reps for 2 sessions, phase up. |

After Week 2: weekly structural review determines exercise swaps, focus rotation, phase changes.

---

## Adjustment Layers

Two layers of change touch the workout plan:

| Layer | Trigger | Scope |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Nightly micro-adjustment | After every workout log | Load/rep targets for next session of that body part only |
| Weekly structural review | "weekly review" or Sunday | Exercise swaps, focus rotation, phase changes, schedule changes |

Micro-adjustments apply the nightly rules and add a changelog line. Structural changes archive the current plan section first.

---

## Nightly Micro-Adjustment Rules

Compare today to the last session that trained this body part:

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| +2 reps on final set for 2 sessions in a row | Bump load OR phase OR round target (pick safest one change) |
| Difficulty 1–2 on all movements AND exceeded round target | Bump focus movement only |
| Difficulty 5 OR form broke on any movement | Mark that movement for de-load (drop one phase, or −10% weight, or −2 rep target) |
| Reps flat for 3 consecutive sessions of same body part | Flag for weekly review — structural change needed, don't keep trying the same thing |

One variable changes per movement per session (reps OR phase OR load — not all three).

---

## Session Sequence (Flexible Rotation)

Sessions follow a fixed rotation regardless of day of week. Train whenever you can — the sequence picks up where it left off.

| Session | Focus |
|---------|-------|
| A | Arms / Chest |
| B | Legs |
| C | Abs |
| D | Back |

**Recovery:** Take a rest or active recovery day after every 2–3 sessions before continuing the rotation. Never do two heavy sessions back to back without recovery between.

**Rotation:** A → B → [recovery] → C → D → [recovery] → repeat

**Fixed schedule option:** Users who prefer specific training days (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri) can map sessions to days in their workout plan. The rotation still applies — just locked to those days.

---

## Cardio and Fat Loss

Cardio and calorie deficit drive fat loss. Strength training preserves muscle. Never substitute extra strength volume for cardio.

Cardio goes on active recovery days or after short strength sessions. Do not run long cardio before strength work.

---

## Substitution Principles

- Form break = drop one phase on that movement, not skip it.
- Equipment unavailable = substitute the closest pattern (pull for pull, push for push, hinge for hinge).
- Resistance bands substitute for any pull movement.
- Pain (not soreness) = remove the movement and flag for weekly review.

---

## Volume Load

Volume Load (VL) measures total training load per movement per session, accounting for both weight and reps.

**Formula:**
- Weighted movement: `VL = weight (lbs) × total reps (rounds × reps per round)`
- Bodyweight movement: `VL = total reps (rounds × reps per round)`

VL shows progress even when individual rep counts plateau. A user doing 5 rounds × 6 reps at 40 lbs = VL 1,200. Next session 5 rounds × 8 reps at 40 lbs = VL 1,600. Clear improvement even though phase didn't change.

Track weekly VL per movement in the Weekly Learnings summary. Week-over-week VL trend is a reliable indicator of progression.

---

## Session Tracking Format

```markdown
| Movement | Weight | Rounds × Reps | Total Reps | VL | Difficulty (1–5) | Notes |
|----------|--------|--------------|------------|-----|-----------------|-------|
```

Plus: total rounds completed, overall session notes.

This data drives nightly micro-adjustment and volume load tracking. Without it the plan cannot adapt.

## Movement Examples by Slot and Phase

Use this when generating a new user's workout plan during setup. Pick based on equipment and experience level. The Focus slot is always the most demanding movement of the session — go heavier or more complex there.

### Arms / Chest Slot

| Phase | Movement Options |
|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Standard push-ups, incline push-ups (hands on bench), knee push-ups |
| 2 | Close-grip push-ups, diamond push-ups, pike push-ups |
| 3 | Archer push-ups, tension push-ups (3-sec eccentric + 1-sec pause), deficit push-ups (hands on books/plates) |

**Focus (heavier press):**
| Phase | Movement Options |
|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | DB floor press (light), push-up to failure |
| 2 | KB floor press (moderate), DB bench press |
| 3 | KB floor press (heavy), barbell bench, weighted push-ups |

---

### Legs Slot

| Phase | Movement Options |
|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Bodyweight squats, goblet squat (light KB), step-ups (bodyweight) |
| 2 | Goblet squat (moderate KB), KB deadlift, weighted step-ups |
| 3 | KB swings (heavy, 2-handed), RDL (KB or barbell), hex bar deadlift |

**Focus (single-leg or high-volume):**
| Phase | Movement Options |
|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Reverse lunges, static lunges (bodyweight) |
| 2 | Bulgarian split squats (bodyweight or light KB), box step-ups (weighted) |
| 3 | Bulgarian split squats (heavy KB), pistol squat progressions, KB swings + weighted step-ups (alternating rounds) |

---

### Abs Slot

| Phase | Movement Options |
|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Crunches, dead bugs, bird dogs, plank (20–30 sec) |
| 2 | Leg raises (flat), plank holds (45–60 sec), plank shoulder taps |
| 3 | Hanging leg raises, toe-to-bars, plank walk-outs |

**Focus (loaded or isometric):**
| Phase | Movement Options |
|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Sit-ups, Russian twists (bodyweight) |
| 2 | KB sit-ups, plank with hip sway (60 sec) |
| 3 | KB sit-up to press, KB overhead marches, hanging bar L-sits |

---

### Back Slot

| Phase | Movement Options |
|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Inverted rows (bar low, body near horizontal), resistance band rows |
| 2 | Inverted rows (feet elevated), DB single-arm rows (moderate) |
| 3 | Pull-ups (bodyweight strict), barbell rows, KB single-arm rows (heavy) |

**Focus (heavy pull or compound):**
| Phase | Movement Options |
|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Band-assisted pull-ups, lat pulldowns (if gym available) |
| 2 | Pull-ups (bodyweight), DB single-arm rows (heavy) |
| 3 | Weighted pull-ups, KB renegade rows (plank position), barbell hang clean and press |

---

### Equipment Scaling Notes

| Equipment | Approach |
|-----------|----------|
| Bodyweight only | Phases 1–2 for most slots. Progress via tempo (slower eccentric), pauses, or range of motion. |
| KB / dumbbells | Full Phase 1–3 available across all slots. |
| Full gym (barbell + rack) | All phases available. Substitute barbell for KB in focus movements for heavier loading. |

When in doubt: start one phase lower than the user thinks they need. Week 1 is calibration — undershooting is recoverable, overshooting causes form breaks.
